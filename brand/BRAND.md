# Brand Identity — IntentMandateGuard

## Visual & Conceptual Positioning

**IntentMandateGuard** represents institutional-grade cryptographic guardrails for autonomous AI operators on GenLayer. The brand combines high-contrast terminal precision with verifiable neuro-symbolic security.

## Design Tokens

* **Primary Background:** Deep Obsidian (`#0B0F17`)
* **Surface Panel:** Midnight Slate (`#131B2E`)
* **Border / Hairline:** Steel Navy (`#233152`)
* **Primary Accent:** Cyber Cyan (`#38BDF8`)
* **Verified / Active State:** Emerald (`#22C55E`)
* **Denied / Halted State:** Crimson (`#EF4444`)

## Typography

* **Headings & UI:** `Inter`, system-ui sans-serif — clean geometric legibility for policy dashboards.
* **Calldata, Hashes & Payloads:** `JetBrains Mono` / monospace — strict character alignment for addresses, nonces, and SHA-256 proof hashes.
