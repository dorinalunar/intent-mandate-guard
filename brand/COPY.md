# Copywriting & Messaging Guide — IntentMandateGuard

## Core Taglines

* **Primary Tagline:** Bounded Semantic Execution Mandates & Single-Use Intent Tickets on GenVM.
* **Short Elevator Pitch:** Delegate operational authority to AI agents without surrendering custody or risking prompt-injected transactions.

## Value Pillars

1. **Natural-Language Guardrails, On-Chain Finality:** Write human-readable treasury and DeFi rules; let GenVM validators enforce them before a ticket is ever activated.
2. **Fail-Closed Certainty:** Every rule must pass with `solid` certainty. Ambiguity never slips through.
3. **Two-Phase Quota Accounting:** Lock weight on ticket issuance, redeem exact SHA-256 anchored payloads at execution time, or reclaim unused quota on expiry.
